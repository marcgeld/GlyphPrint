# Fixture provenance

- `px10-cover-observations.json`: user-reported cover/paper observations with
  USB power conditions recorded where specified. Open cover and closed cover
  without paper both returned `001629`. Closed cover with paper and USB power
  disconnected returned `000a28`; the earlier closed-cover sample was `000329`
  with paper and USB conditions unspecified. No individual status flag is
  established. Full frames were not supplied or reconstructed.
- `px10-status.json`: observed PX10 GATT services and a real A3 response captured
  over CoreBluetooth on macOS on 2026-09-09. The capture performed a state query
  without printing. Payload `000a27` is preserved without assigning unverified
  meanings to its bits.
- `reference-packets.json`: independent protocol vectors, not hardware captures.
  Lattice/raster framing and receive-ready are from rbaron/catprinter. The pause
  frame is also documented in xome4ok/cat-printer/gb01print.py.

Do not replace captures with output generated by GlyphPrint. New hardware
observations should record the device, date, command, raw response, and known
physical conditions. A successful BLE write is not a confirmed physical print.
- `px10-print-notifications.json`: actual decoded notifications exported by
  `gprint --printer <PX10 UUID> --trace ... "GlyphPrint status test"` on 2026-09-09,
  using a 10 ms write interval. The job returned `submittedToBluetooth`; no
  recognized receive-ready frame followed the final write. Physical output
  quality was not confirmed as part of this capture. The A8 response is retained
  as unknown rather than assigning unverified meanings to its fields.
